Mini project — 3D & motion

Boat Soke.

Blender  |  After Effects

A stylized 3D scene created in Blender, featuring an original robot character aboard a small search boat. The project explores low-poly modeling, lighting, composition and visual storytelling. The final scene was animated and assembled into a short looping sequence in After Effects.

Context
In-house Project
Tools
Blender, After Effects
Discipline
3D & motion design
Year
2026

01 — Final render

A small boat,
out searching.

The scene keeps its shapes simple so the light can do the work — a low-poly hull, flat water, and a single warm sign against a cold blue.

02 — The loop

Eight seconds,
on repeat.

The rendered sequence was assembled and graded in After Effects into a short silent loop. Press play — it holds still until you ask it to move.

03 — Under the surface

Built from
few faces.

Low-poly is a discipline as much as a style: every face has to earn its place. The wireframe shows how little geometry the scene actually carries.
